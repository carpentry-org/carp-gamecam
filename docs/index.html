carp-gamecam

A 3D perspective camera for Carp.

(load "git@github.com:carpentry-org/carp-gamecam@master")
  • Camera — position and orientation held as basis vectors, with yaw/pitch for fly-cam navigation, producing view and projection matrices
  • CameraMat4 — a 4x4 matrix, column-major

Matrices come out column-major, which is what WGPU and the other modern graphics APIs want, so they can go straight into a uniform buffer with no transpose. Vectors are carp's own Vector3.
